## Project Snapshot
- **Product:** Ruff Day Mobile Grooming — a self-contained mobile dog-grooming van in Austin, TX that comes to your home (full groom: wash, trim, nails, ears).
- **Price / offer:** $75 standard groom.
- **Campaign goal (fixed):** Get 50 bookings in month one.
- **Tone (fixed):** Friendly, warm, and a little playful — dogs are family, and grooming shouldn't be stressful for the dog or the owner.
- **Channels:** Instagram + Facebook organic posts, plus one WhatsApp message to a ~300-person pet-expo waitlist.

## 1. Audience Insights
_(Research Agent)_

- **Primary:** Austin dog owners who treat dogs as family — busy households, dual-income couples, young professionals, older/less-mobile owners; located within the van's tight service radius.
- **Warm sub-segment:** ~300 WhatsApp waitlist from a pet expo (high intent).
- **Secondary:** Owners of anxious, elderly, reactive, hard-to-transport dogs (and multi-dog homes) — low-stress at-home grooming is the deciding factor.
- **What they care about:** Dog's comfort/wellbeing above all; reclaiming time (no drive/drop-off/pickup); predictability & trust in letting a stranger groom their "family member" at home.
- **Pain points:** Stress of the traditional trip (car wrestling, cross-town drive, nervous dog in a loud shop, cage time, double trip); distressing for anxious/senior dogs; time-poor owners can't fit shop hours.
- **Triggers:** Dog overdue; past bad shop experience; life-stage change; one-tap booking; warm waitlist personal nudge; founding-customer "get in early" feeling.
- **Hesitations:** Brand new with ZERO reviews — trust is the biggest barrier; van logistics questions; is $75 worth it; coverage on their street.
- **Key insight:** Convert on convenience, low-stress, and transparency — not on reviews.
