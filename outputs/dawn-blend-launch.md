# Final Campaign Package — Dawn Blend Launch

**Goal (unchanged):** Sell 300 bags in launch week.
**Tone (unchanged):** Calm, honest, slightly nerdy about coffee — no hype, no "world's best" claims.
**Channels:** Organic social + one WhatsApp broadcast (~800 subscribers).

---

## 1. Audience Insights
_(Research Agent)_

- **Primary audience:** Remote / work-from-home professionals, roughly 28–45, who drink coffee daily and treat the morning cup as the small ritual that starts the workday. They care about quality but don't own (or want to fuss with) espresso gear.
- **What they care about:** A reliably good cup with minimal effort; feeling a bit more "intentional" about a daily habit; knowing where their coffee comes from without needing to be an expert.
- **Pain points:** Café runs cost time and money now that they work from home; grocery-store bags are inconsistent and anonymous; "specialty" coffee often feels intimidating or over-hyped.
- **Triggers to act:** A simple brew method they already own (drip, pour-over, French press) works; honest, plain descriptions instead of marketing gloss; a small, low-risk first purchase.
- **Reasons to hesitate:** $18 feels like a premium vs. supermarket coffee; skepticism toward hype; uncertainty about roast/flavor fit.
- **Assumptions & unknowns:** `[ASSUMPTION]` audience already brews at home with basic equipment. `[NEEDS DATA]` actual WhatsApp list engagement rate and past purchase behavior.

**Handoff to Strategy Agent:** Lead with *effortless quality* and *honest transparency* — this audience wants a better daily cup without barista fuss or hype, and price resistance is best answered with plain value, not discounts.

---

## 2. Campaign Strategy
_(Strategy Agent)_

- **Objective:** Sell 300 bags during the 7-day launch window, driven by organic social and one WhatsApp broadcast to ~800 subscribers.
- **Main message:** *A better morning cup, made simple — single-origin light roast for people who work from home.*
- **Content pillars:**
  1. **Effortless mornings** — great coffee with the gear you already have; no barista, no fuss.
  2. **Honest & traceable** — single-origin, plainly described; we tell you what it is, not that it's "the best."
  3. **The work-from-home ritual** — a small, intentional upgrade to the start of the workday.
  4. **Direct & fair** — $18 a bag, shipped straight to you, no middleman markup.

**Handoff to Content Agent:** Write social posts that each own one pillar (no overlap) plus one WhatsApp message; every piece expresses the main message and ties back to the 300-bag goal, in the calm/honest/nerdy tone.
