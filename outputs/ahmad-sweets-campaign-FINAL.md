## 1. Audience Insights
_(Research Agent)_

**Core Audience:** Occasion-driven gift buyers across Pakistan celebrating life milestones (weddings, births, exams, achievements) who value trust, tradition, and elegant presentation.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Not daily purchasers, but concentrated buyers during specific celebrations
- Trust-seeking: prefer established brands with proven track records
- Quality and elegance conscious: willing to pay premium prices (Rs. 850/kg) for beautiful packaging and authentic quality
- Price-sensitive for out-of-city delivery: awareness of delivery tiers (Rs. 300–5,000) shapes order decisions
- Multi-channel and convenience-focused: digitally aware but comfortable with traditional phone/WhatsApp ordering

**What They Value:**
- Authentic tradition and family heritage
- Elegant packaging suited for gifting
- Ease of purchase through personal, simple ordering methods
- Trust built over time

**Barriers:**
- Delivery cost concerns outside Khanewal city
- Need for assurance on quality and authenticity before purchase
- Preference for established, proven brands over unknowns

**Platform Presence:**
- Instagram & Facebook: social discovery, celebration inspiration, visual appeal
- WhatsApp broadcast list: already demonstrated reach; direct, personal communication
- Phone/cell calls: trusted, traditional ordering channel for this market

**Strategy Recommendation:** Focus messaging on 15 years of trusted tradition and home-style quality. Highlight elegant gifting presentation and ease of ordering. For out-of-city customers, address delivery clarity and bulk-discount benefits upfront. Use Instagram/Facebook for visual storytelling and celebration moments; WhatsApp and phone for quick, friendly ordering with transparent delivery costs.
